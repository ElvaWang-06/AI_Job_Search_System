## Daily Distribution

There is no fixed quota for each job family.

Always prioritize overall fit and job quality over maintaining a balanced distribution across categories.

Suggested weighting when many strong-fit roles are available:

* Tier 1 — approximately half of daily recommendations
* Tier 2 — approximately one-third
* Tier 3 — the remainder

Tiers express search priority, not exclusivity. A Tier 3 role with unusually strong alignment outranks a weak Tier 1 role.

## Experience Level

**Include:**

* Entry level
* Coordinator
* Associate
* Specialist
* Analyst
* Approximately 0–3 years of required experience
* Roles asking for 3–5 years only when the responsibilities remain early-career and the role is unusually well aligned

**Exclude:**

* Director
* Head of
* Principal
* Lead
* Staff-level titles
* Senior Manager
* Manager, when the role owns a function or team
* Roles clearly requiring staff management
* Roles clearly requiring more than five years of specialized experience

**Do not exclude:**

* Associate Manager
* Assistant Manager
* Manager titles where the responsibilities described are individual-contributor and the stated experience requirement is 0–3 years

## Posting Freshness

**Prioritize:**

* Jobs posted within the past seven days

**Allow:**

* Jobs posted within the past thirty days when the exact listing remains active and relevant

**Exclude:**

* Listings posted more than thirty days ago
* Closed or expired listings
* Listings that are no longer accepting applications
* Listings without an accessible direct job-posting page from the permitted source
* Clearly recycled or inactive listings
* Listings whose stated application deadline has passed
* Listings whose active status cannot be verified

---

## Source Quality

**Primary:** Official careers pages of companies in the target company list
**Secondary:** LinkedIn Jobs, for roles outside the target company list

Do not use Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor, Google Jobs, staffing-agency boards, job aggregators or other third-party sources — for discovery or as an application link.

The target company list sets search priority but is not an exclusive restriction.

## Job Discovery and Link Requirements

### Search Order

1. Read the target job families and the target company list.
2. Read `job_tracker.xlsx`.
3. Search the official careers pages of target companies, categories A–F.
4. Use LinkedIn Jobs for additional roles from target job families.
6. Apply Duplicate Checking, then Active-Status Verification, to every remaining candidate.


### Link Requirements

Every recommendation carries one source label, and the URL must match it.

**`Target Company Careers Page`** — for companies on the target list.
Provide the direct official employer or ATS requisition URL. It must open the specific job, not a general careers page or search-results page. A LinkedIn URL is not required, and a valid target-company role is never rejected for lacking one.

**`LinkedIn Jobs`** — for companies outside the target list.
The role must be discovered through LinkedIn Jobs, and the URL must be the direct posting in the format `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/[JOB-ID]/`: containing the numeric job ID, opening the individual posting, matching the recommended company and position, and currently active.

For LinkedIn-sourced roles, do not substitute a search-results page, company jobs page, collection or recommendation page, shortened URL, employer careers-page link, or a Greenhouse / Lever / Workday / Ashby / SmartRecruiters ATS link. If no valid direct LinkedIn posting URL exists, exclude the role and find another.

**Never construct, guess or fabricate a job ID or URL.** If the real URL cannot be retrieved, the role does not go in the report.

### Duplicate Checking

Check every candidate against `job_tracker.xlsx` using all available identifying information: company · position title · location · requisition ID · LinkedIn job ID · application URL.

Treat as a duplicate when the company and position are substantially the same, even if the title wording differs, the location format differs, the role surfaced through another source, the company reposted the opening, or the application URL changed.

Exclude jobs already recorded in the tracker, previously rejected or archived jobs, recently presented jobs, and reposted versions of previously recorded roles.

### Active-Status Verification

Every recommended job must be confirmed active when checked.

Common to all sources: the exact posting page opens; the company, title and location match; the listing does not state that the job is unavailable or that applications are closed; the page has not been removed; the URL does not redirect to a search page, general careers page, or unrelated position; the stated application deadline has not passed.

Additionally, for target-company roles: an active Apply button or application form is present.

Exclude anything expired, inaccessible, removed, closed, or unverifiable.

---

## Hard Exclusions

Roles that fail any filter elsewhere in this file — job family, experience level, location, compensation, posting freshness, work authorization, duplicate checking, active status, or link requirements — are excluded. This list covers only what is not stated elsewhere:

* Staffing-agency reposts without a named employer
* Sales-heavy roles disguised as recruiting
* Roles whose core qualifications clearly exceed the user's background
* Fabricated jobs or application links

---

## Selection Principles

Recommend only the strongest overall matches after applying all filters and hard exclusions.

Rank by:

1. target companies, categories A–F
2. Roles from the broader LinkedIn Jobs search
3. Tier and sub-track priority
4. Overall career alignment
5. Posting freshness
6. Compensation fit
7. Experience-level fit

Prioritize job quality over category balance or filling a quota.

**Never lower the matching, freshness, verification or link-quality requirements to reach a fixed number of recommendations.** A list of verified matches is the correct output; a list containing weak or unverified roles is a failure.
